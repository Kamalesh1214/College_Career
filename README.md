# SVCE Placement Intelligence Hub

A web-based **Company Research & Placement Analytics Portal** designed for students of **Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering (SVCE)**.

The platform helps students explore companies, understand placement categories, and access structured company intelligence to support better preparation for campus placements.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://college-career.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **SVCE Placement Intelligence Hub** is designed to organize and present company-related information in a structured and accessible way.

Instead of searching for company information across multiple sources, students can use the platform to explore companies, industries, locations, and placement categories from a centralized interface.

The project focuses on combining:

- Company Research
- Placement Analytics
- Structured Data
- Database Integration
- Search and Filtering
- Modern User Interface

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 Search companies by name, industry, or location
- 🏢 Explore a structured company directory
- 📊 Browse companies based on placement tiers
- 🎯 Categorization of companies into placement levels
- 💼 Company intelligence and research information
- 🗂 Structured company data
- 📱 Responsive user interface
- ⚡ Fast and modern frontend experience

---

## 🎯 Placement Categories

The platform organizes companies into different placement categories:

- Super Dream
- Dream
- Standard
- Regular

This helps students quickly identify and explore companies based on placement tiers.
